Comprendre les indicateurs

Prix moyen (VWAP) et impact

Le VWAP est le prix moyen obtenu en consommant les ordres visibles, avec une quantité cible correspondant approximativement au nominal choisi. L'impact compare ce VWAP au meilleur prix du même côté du carnet. Il n'est pas une mesure complète du spread d'entrée entre les deux plateformes.

Funding horaire et direction DN

Le short reçoit lorsque le funding est positif. On shorte le taux le plus haut et on longe le taux le plus bas. Gain brut théorique = nominal × différence des taux × nombre d'heures, si taux et notionnels restent stables.

Pourquoi un coût de sortie réservé ?

Le carnet futur est inconnu. La V4 réserve un montant configurable pour le slippage de sortie, mais n'invente pas le prix ou le carnet de fermeture.

Statistique de stabilité du funding

Le bouton historique compare les derniers points disponibles sur 24 h et calcule moyenne, minimum, maximum et écart-type. Cela ne permet pas de prédire le funding futur ni d'assurer la constance des taux.

Limites des alias de sous-jacents

Un ticker peut avoir des noms différents selon les DEX. Les alias proposés sont expérimentaux ; vérifie manuellement oracle, unité, multiplicateur, horaires et nature du contrat. L'outil n'affirme pas que GOLD et XAU sont interchangeables.

Guide & methodology — Entropy Intelligence Web 5.4

This read-only application compares native Entropy io: perps with Hyperliquid/HIP-3, Extended, Arcus and Variational. Data can be unavailable, stale or not comparable. A positive row is not a recommendation to trade.

Calculation glossary

Funding / h

Current hourly decimal rate shown as %. The usual convention is positive = long pays short. Variational converts last.interval_rate / (interval_seconds / 3600). Current rates are not guaranteed to persist.

Funding projected

Per-leg notional × absolute hourly funding difference × hours × persistence factor. It represents a constant-exposure approximation, not realized funding.

Round-trip fees

Two entry and two exit orders. Formula: 2 × notional × (Entropy fee + opposite-venue fee). Fees are manually configurable assumptions; Variational RFQ costs generally enter through bid/ask quotes.

Exit slip. reserve

Assumed exit execution deterioration: 2 × per-leg notional × the configured slippage %. Not an observed charge. Entry impact and RFQ spread are not captured in the ranking.

12h / 24h indicative balance

Projected funding − estimated round-trip fees − exit slippage reserve. This excludes entry spread, mark basis convergence/divergence, changing funding, liquidation and possible time-of-day trading restrictions.

Break-even hours

Estimated fees and exit reserve ÷ projected funding dollars per hour. Infinite if the projected funding is zero. It does not include entry costs.

Mark basis

(Entropy mark / other mark) − 1. This identifies reference-price differences; mark prices are not execution prices and contracts may reference different underlyings.

OI / 24h volume

Open interest and traded volume are filters, not an indication of executable size. Variational reports long + short trader OI and therefore may not be comparable across venues.

RFQ / Variational

Variational provides indicative bid and ask quotes at 1k, 100k and 1m. For a 5k trade, the 100k tier is not a 5k executable price. No fictitious L2 depth is constructed.

Critical flags

Unverified ticker alias, price basis exceeding the threshold, closed or restricted market, stale data, or missing required figures. A flag is a research prompt, not a validated statistical risk score.

Paired funding history (72 h)

For HIP-3, press “Paired funding history” on a selected pair to fetch real aligned hourly data. The funding history column then shows observation count, favorable-hour share, historic funding/day, and current-rate funding/day. This is historical context, not a forecast or execution P&L. External venues have no paired history in this release.

Using this scanner responsibly

  1. Check that the instruments represent the same economic exposure and share compatible trading sessions.
  2. Set realistic fees for each venue; inspect the market's bid/ask and executable size manually when no L2 exists.
  3. Compare both 12h and 24h indicative nets and test lower funding persistence.
  4. Review mark basis and historical stability; a large mark difference is not automatically an arbitrage opportunity.
  5. Do not publish a trade recommendation based solely on this dashboard.
